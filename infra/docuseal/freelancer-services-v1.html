Terms

  1. Services. The Contractor will perform tasks for the Role listed above as assigned through the Company's task system. Each task states its deliverable and acceptance criteria before work starts.
  2. Independent contractor. The Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent of the Company. The Contractor decides how and when to do the work, provides their own equipment, and is responsible for their own taxes.
  3. Fees. The Company pays the Rate above for each accepted unit of work. The Contractor will not be paid for work that does not meet the task's acceptance criteria, but may revise and resubmit it.
  4. Payment. Payment is made in cryptocurrency from the Company's NEAR treasury, or on another supported chain through NEAR Intents, to an address the Contractor has verified by signing a challenge with its key. The Contractor is responsible for keeping that address and its keys secure; a payment sent to the verified address is final. Payments may be held for the owner's approval or deferred to the next calendar month when the Company's monthly payout limit is reached; a deferred payment remains owed.
  5. Tax form. No payment is made until the Contractor has completed the applicable tax form (W-9 for US persons, W-8BEN otherwise), which the Company will send separately.
  6. Work product. On payment, the Contractor assigns to the Company all rights in the deliverables created under this agreement, except pre-existing material and open-source components, for which the Contractor grants the Company a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use them as part of the deliverables.
  7. Confidentiality. The Contractor will keep the Company's non-public information confidential, use it only to perform the services, and return or delete it when this agreement ends.
  8. Performance review. The Contractor's work is reviewed against the key performance indicators of the Role. Reviews are carried out by software agents; the Contractor may ask for a review by the Company's owner by replying to any notice.
  9. Automated communication. The Contractor acknowledges that many messages, interviews and reviews from the Company come from AI agents, which will identify themselves as such.
  10. Term and termination. This agreement continues until either party ends it by written notice (email is enough). The Company will pay for all work accepted before the notice date.
  11. Personal data. The Company processes the Contractor's personal data only to administer this agreement and keeps it no longer than needed for that and for legal record keeping.
  12. Entire agreement; law. This is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject. It is governed by the laws of __GOVERNING_LAW__. Changes must be in writing and accepted by both parties.
